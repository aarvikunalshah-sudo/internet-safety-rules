Password safety

learn how to keep your passwords safe on the internet.

Passwords are like keys that protect your online accounts, so it is important to keep them safe. Always create strong passwords using a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Never share your passwords with friends or strangers, and avoid using easy passwords like your name or birthday. Try to use a different password for each account, and never write them where other people can find them. If someone asks for your password online, do not share it. Remember, keeping your passwords secret helps protect your personal information and keeps your accounts safe!
