Welcome

The internet is a great place to learn, play games, and connect with others, but it is important to stay safe while using it. Internet safety means protecting yourself by creating strong passwords, keeping them secret, and never sharing personal information like your address, phone number, or passwords with strangers. You should also be careful of online scams, avoid clicking on suspicious links, and never open messages from people you don't trust. Always think before you click, be careful about what you share, and remember to tell a trusted adult if something online makes you feel uncomfortable. Making smart choices online helps keep you and your information safe!
