The internet is full of helpful websites, but sometimes people use it to trick others with online scams. To stay safe, be careful of messages, emails, or websites that promise free prizes, money, or gifts that seem too good to be true. Never click on suspicious links or download files from people you don't know. Scammers may pretend to be someone you trust to steal your personal information or passwords. Always double-check before sharing information or clicking on anything unfamiliar. Remember, if something seems strange or too good to be true, stop and ask a trusted adult for help!
