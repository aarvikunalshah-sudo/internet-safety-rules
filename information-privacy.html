Keeping your personal information private on the internet is very important because not everyone online can be trusted. Never share private details like your full name, home address, phone number, school name, or passwords with strangers. Be careful about what you post on social media, because once something is online, it can be difficult to remove. Always check your privacy settings to make sure only trusted people can see your information. Avoid sharing pictures or personal details with people you don't know. Remember, think before you post and always ask a trusted adult if you are unsure about sharing something online!
