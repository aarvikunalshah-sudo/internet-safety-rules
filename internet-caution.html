Being cautious on the internet means thinking carefully before clicking, sharing, or downloading anything online. Always be careful when visiting new websites, talking to strangers, or opening unknown messages. Avoid clicking on suspicious links or downloading things that you do not recognize. Remember that not everything you see online is true, and some people may pretend to be someone they are not. If something makes you feel uncomfortable or unsafe, stop what you are doing and tell a trusted adult. Always think before you click and make smart choices to protect yourself online!
